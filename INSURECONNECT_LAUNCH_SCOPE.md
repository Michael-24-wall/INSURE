# INSURECONNECT — Launch Scope (Release 1.0)

Date: 2026-11-17

## Available at launch (IMPLEMENTED AND VERIFIED)

- **Motor insurance** only: comprehensive, third party, third party/fire/theft,
  commercial, motorcycle — full quotation → application → underwriting → payment →
  issuance → certificate → claims → premium/commission ledger.
- Registration/auth for customer, insurer (provider), dealer, agent, inspector,
  claims officer, platform admin.
- Business network (insurer↔dealer partnerships, consent, commissions).
- Finance: premium/commission/refund double-entry ledger (claim/insurer settlement
  postings pending approval).

## Not available at launch (correctly labelled "Expanding")

Health & Medical, Life, Business & Commercial, Property & Home, Travel,
Marine & Cargo, Agriculture, Personal Accident, Liability — the UI shows these as
"Expanding" and produces **no** fake quotes/policies. They are not to be offered
until their quotation, underwriting, policy, payment and claims workflows are built
and tested.

## Positioning disclaimer (must be maintained)

INSURECONNECT operates as a **marketplace / technology intermediary**, not as an
insurer or underwriter. All policies are issued by licensed insurer partners. No
regulatory or licensing approval is claimed.

## Regulatory / professional confirmations required before launch (REQUIRES EXTERNAL CONFIRMATION)

1. Insurance intermediary/marketplace licensing applicable to the operating
   jurisdiction(s).
2. Data-protection / privacy (customer PII, KYC, health/financial data).
3. Insurer and dealer partner agreements and underwriting authority.
4. Payment provider (Daraja/Flutterwave) terms and settlement accounts.
5. Customer terms of service and privacy policy reviewed by legal counsel.
6. KYC/AML obligations for policy issuance and claim payouts.

None of the above is confirmed as obtained; they are professional/regulatory items to
be completed before go-live.
